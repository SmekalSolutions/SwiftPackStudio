PRIVACY POLICY

SwiftPack Studio
Published by Smekal Solutions

Version: 1.1

This Policy explains what personal data Smekal Solutions ("we", "us") collects through SwiftPack Studio and why.

1. SWIFTPACK STUDIO DATA COLLECTION

SwiftPack Studio is provided as a free and open-source Windows desktop tool, licensed under the MIT License.

The Software does not require a paid license, subscription, trial registration, account, or online activation to use the Software.

SwiftPack Studio is designed to perform application packaging and deployment work on your own computer and, where applicable, within your own Microsoft Intune tenant.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

Smekal Solutions does not require you to provide personal data to install or use the free SwiftPack Studio software.

The Software does not require an account, registration, login, password, or license-server authentication for normal use.

If you voluntarily contact Smekal Solutions by e-mail or through another support channel (including by posting issues, pull requests, or discussions on the public GitHub repository, which are public and subject to GitHub's own terms and privacy policy), we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, e-mail address, company name, enquiry, support request, or attachments.

3. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT THROUGH SWIFTPACK STUDIO

SwiftPack Studio is designed to operate as a local desktop application. Smekal Solutions does not operate a backend server, application server, database, or telemetry service that receives or stores data generated by SwiftPack Studio during normal use.

SwiftPack Studio does not collect, transmit to Smekal Solutions, or store on Smekal Solutions servers:

- application installers or software packages you work with;
- scripts, packaging configurations, or deployment settings you create or use;
- your Microsoft Intune credentials or authentication tokens;
- your Microsoft Intune tenant data or content published to Intune;
- hardware identifiers, serial numbers, or machine names;
- usage analytics or telemetry describing how you use SwiftPack Studio features; or
- your files or application content processed by the Software.

Packaging and other processing performed by SwiftPack Studio takes place on your computer and, where applicable, within your own Microsoft tenant or third-party services that you choose to use.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

SwiftPack Studio may interact with third-party software, services, repositories, package sources, Microsoft Intune, GitHub (including the public source repository), winget, e-mail services, or other services that you configure or use.

Those third parties operate independently of Smekal Solutions and may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Smekal Solutions does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

5. APPLICATION PACKAGING AND RELATED SERVICES

If you separately contact or engage Smekal Solutions for application packaging, migration, testing, training, technical support, customization, implementation, consulting, integration, troubleshooting, or other professional services, we may process business contact details and materials that you voluntarily provide for that engagement.

Such information may include your name, work e-mail, company, enquiry or quote request, application installers, scripts, packaging configurations, requirements, and related documentation.

We use such information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, deliver the requested service, provide support, and manage the business relationship.

Materials supplied for a service engagement are used for the agreed service and handled according to the applicable service agreement and confidentiality obligations.

6. SHARING

We do not sell personal data.

Information voluntarily provided to Smekal Solutions may be shared with service providers or subcontractors where reasonably necessary to provide an agreed service, subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.

7. RETENTION

For direct enquiries and professional services, we retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant business relationship, service delivery, security, legal, tax, accounting, or record-keeping requirements.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, update, or delete personal data, withdraw consent where applicable, and lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

To exercise applicable rights or ask privacy questions, contact us at contact@smekalsolutions.com.

9. SECURITY

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information that we receive directly from users or customers.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. CHILDREN

SwiftPack Studio is a professional software tool and is not directed to children or intended for anyone under the age of 18.

11. CHANGES

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will revise the effective date when changes are made. Material changes may be communicated through appropriate channels.
